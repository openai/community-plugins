# Dimensional references and sensor setup

The sensor station accepts the existing tabletop enclosure through its two external mounting ears. Its assembly in this fixture is represented by a mechanical envelope derived from the revised enclosure STEP. The physical enclosure, accelerometer, cable and recording instrument are supplied by the operator.

| Interface from the revised STEP files | Measured in CAD |
| --- | --- |
| Quad frame, four guard bores | 16.30 mm inner radius |
| Quad frame, four cylindrical rims | 5.50 mm tall, from Z = 7.00 to 12.50 mm in the aircraft coordinate system |
| Revised guard outer radius and radial wall | 17.70 mm and 1.40 mm |
| Enclosure mounting ears | Two vertical Ø3.40 mm through bores, 64.00 mm apart, through 3.00 mm of material |
| Enclosure footprint with ears | 73.00 × 36.00 mm |
| Removable lid | 52.00 × 36.00 mm, from 15.00 to 17.20 mm above the enclosure seating surface |
| Lid fasteners | Four axes at X = ±21 mm and Y = ±13 mm relative to the enclosure center. Screw heads reach 18.65 mm above the seating surface. |

The new coupon design uses the frame's inner radius and axial height. Its free arc, root attachment and loading feature belong to the bench experiment. The 1.00 mm coupon represents the original nominal radial wall and the 1.40 mm coupon represents the revision.

Secure the accelerometer board inside the enclosure and record which sensor axis points along the fixture's loading direction. Route the cable with the same slack and strain relief in every run. Motion at the sensor includes the effects of its board mounting, case and connection to the plate. Check the accelerometer's range, bandwidth, sample rate, units and mounting orientation before comparing recorded waveforms.
